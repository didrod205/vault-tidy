FAQ

How do I activate Pro?

After checkout your license key appears on the confirmation page. You can recover it anytime on the Support page with your email and order ID. In Obsidian open Settings → Vault Tidy → Enter license key.

What data is sent to the AI?

Only tag names with counts, or the title and first 1,500 characters of the notes you choose to tag. Nothing is stored and nothing is used for training.

Can I cancel?
