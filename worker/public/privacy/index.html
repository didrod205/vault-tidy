Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what data Vault Tidy ("we", E:LAB) processes.

Free plugin

The free features run entirely on your device. No data leaves your computer, and there is no telemetry or analytics.

Pro features

When you use an AI feature, the plugin sends to our API (hosted on Cloudflare) only: tag names with usage counts, or the title and the first 1,500 characters of the notes you selected. This content is processed by Cloudflare Workers AI to produce suggestions and is not stored or used for training. We keep a daily usage counter per license for fair use.

Licensing and payments

Payments are processed by Paddle.com as Merchant of Record; see Paddle's privacy policy. We receive your email, country and subscription status to issue and validate your license key, and store a random device identifier for each activation.

Retention

License records are kept while your subscription is active and up to 2 years after for tax and support purposes. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data by emailing hello@elab-studio.com.
